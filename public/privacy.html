Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What AnimeHub is

AnimeHub is an online discovery catalog and indexing tool for anime movies and television series. AnimeHub does not host, upload, or stream video media files.

Information this site uses

  • Visitors who are not signed in can save a watchlist in this browser's local storage. If you create an account and choose to sync that watchlist, its anime IDs are stored in your Supabase account's cloud watchlist.
  • If you create an account, Supabase processes your email address and authentication data. AnimeHub stores a profile and the cloud watchlist associated with your account.
  • When you search, your search term is sent to AnimeHub's server and forwarded to AniList to retrieve catalog metadata. AniList receives the query to return matching titles. Review AniList's terms and privacy information.
  • External trailer links point to YouTube, and licensed streaming search queries link to JustWatch. These external services operate under their respective privacy policies.
  • The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser makes standard requests to load font assets.
  • Server hosting providers may maintain standard technical access logs (such as IP address, request timestamp, and user-agent) for site security and operational stability.

Analytics and advertising

AnimeHub does not use advertising trackers, analytics tracking scripts, or payment processing.

Your choices

You may remove saved titles from your local or cloud watchlist at any time. You may request permanent account deletion from the Profile page when account deletion is configured on the server. The server verifies your sign-in session and deletes the authenticated Supabase user; associated profile and cloud watchlist records are removed through database cascades. If deletion is unavailable, contact us using the contact page. Removing this site's local storage clears browser-stored watchlist data but does not delete a cloud account.
